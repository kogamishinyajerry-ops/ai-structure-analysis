## Geometry

100 mm × 50 mm × 5 mm plate with a 10 mm-radius circular hole in the
centre. Defined in `data/plate_with_hole.geo` (gmsh OpenCASCADE
geometry kernel).

| parameter | symbol | value | unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| plate length | L | 0.100 | m |
| plate width | W | 0.050 | m |
| plate thickness | T | 0.005 | m |
| hole radius | R | 0.010 | m |

Coordinate frame: x = long axis (0..L), y = short axis (0..W), z =
thickness (0..T). Origin at one corner.

## Canonical BC + load (Phase 21 A runner default)

* clamp: `PlanarSelection(axis="x", value_m=0.0, tol_m=1e-5)` —
  all nodes on the x=0 face fully restrained (DOF 1-3).
* load: `PlanarSelection(axis="x", value_m=L, tol_m=1e-5)`, DOF=1,
  total_force_n = +250 N — tensile uniaxial pull along x.

Under this loading the far-field stress σ_∞ = F/(W·T) = 250/(0.05·
0.005) = 1.0e6 Pa. The Howland tabulation at 2a/W = 2·0.010/0.050 =
0.4 gives K = 3.740, so the analytical hole-edge peak σ ≈ 3.74 MPa.
(The peak is the classical Kirsch K=3 lifted slightly by the
finite-strip geometry; see `plate_kirsch.py` for the tabulation.)

## Phase 21 A residual envelope (honest C3D4 hole-edge convergence)

The Phase 21 A runner lands a -10.71% residual at characteristic mesh
length 0.003 m. The hole edge is a high-gradient stress field; linear
C3D4 tets under-predict the peak by 15-25% per Pilkey commentary
without adaptive refinement, so the observed -10.7% is consistent with
the documented C3D4 convergence behaviour. Tolerance is set to 20%;
tightening below 15% needs adaptive mesh refinement (Phase 22+ scope)
or C3D10 quadratic tets.
